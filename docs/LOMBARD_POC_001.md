# EarthForge ? Lombard Street POC 001

## Target

The iconic 1000 block of Lombard Street between Hyde Street and Leavenworth Street, San Francisco. The project intentionally proves terrain and public hardscape before any house architecture is allowed into the build.

## Locked frame

- Anchor: Hyde / Lombard centerline intersection.
- +X east, +Z south.
- 1 meter = 1 Minecraft block.
- NAVD88 vertical zero: 85.869 m at the Hyde control.
- Leavenworth control: X 144.512 m, Z -25.686 m.
- Measured endpoint drop: **32.85 m**.
- Straight control distance: **146.78 m**.
- Straight endpoint grade: **22.38%**.
- Crooked OSM centerline: **197.39 m / 157 nodes**.

## L1 hardscape v001

The first review schematic contains no houses. It includes:

- 2010 NOAA/USGS LiDAR terrain with 1/16-block top resolution;
- brick switchback roadway from the dense OSM centerline;
- first-pass source-derived curbs;
- mapped brick/concrete stairs and footways;
- mapped hedge massing;
- Hyde and Leavenworth intersection context;
- Hyde cable-car rail alignment;
- permanent off-site loading pad.

Validation: exact block readback PASS, exact Astra cell readback PASS. Astra hosts: 13,067; occupied microcells: 24,437,240.

## Gate

Do **not** add houses yet. First load L1 in Minecraft and judge the hill, switchback width/shape, vertical rhythm, stair relationships and intersection scale. Low planter/retaining-wall detail is the next hardscape pass after that review.
