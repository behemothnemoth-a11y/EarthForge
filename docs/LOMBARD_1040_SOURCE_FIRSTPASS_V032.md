# 1040 Lombard source-led first pass v032

v031 proved registration, but it deliberately underused EarthForge's available source package. v032 is the first genuinely architectural review pass after the reset.

## Source basis

High-confidence controls remain the DataSF source footprint, the accepted v024 site/driveway relationship, and the source absolute vertical envelope.

The richer front hierarchy is derived from a dedicated multi-year photo set (2008, 2009, two 2013 views, 2014, 2019). The 2008 near-frontal view is used for candidate proportional subdivisions, while the oblique views cross-check depth ordering and persistent topology.

The tracked measurement package is:
`projects/lombard_sf/source_manifests/1040_lombard_photo_measurements_v032.json`.

## What this pass builds

- entire old 1040 placeholder/v020 interpretation removed across the source footprint;
- full source-footprint side/rear body shell only to the terrace/body-top level;
- recessed left entry;
- street garage and surround;
- broad lower projecting central bay;
- upper central bay set back from the lower bay;
- broad right vertical body rather than the rejected narrow v026 strip;
- grouped multi-lite window fields and major dark framing;
- projecting terrace fascia;
- open railing;
- open pergola reaching the source upper envelope.

## Critical correction from v026/v031

The enclosed body no longer runs to the top of the source first-return envelope. It stops at the photo-derived terrace deck/body-top band. Only the open rooftop structure reaches the upper source envelope.

This prevents the giant hollow-fence reading of v031 and the wrong vertical hierarchy of v026.

## Confidence

- footprint/registering: high structured source;
- absolute driveway-to-top envelope: medium-high source control;
- visible vertical/horizontal facade subdivisions: medium photo-proportioned and cross-checked;
- projection/recess depths: low-medium, cross-checked by oblique photos;
- final rear/roof architecture: unresolved.

No interior geometry is claimed.

## Review gate

Review the overall architecture, not fine detail. Specifically judge:

1. whole-house scale and silhouette;
2. garage relationship to the road;
3. lower bay versus upper bay depth/order;
4. width of the right vertical body;
5. recessed left entry;
6. terrace fascia, open railing and pergola;
7. unchanged public realm/context.

Do not refine small trim until this massing/facade hierarchy passes Minecraft review.
