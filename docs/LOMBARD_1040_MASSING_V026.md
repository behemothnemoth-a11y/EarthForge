## Candidate 3D dimensions
Photo perspective strongly establishes relative depth but does not survey it. For this review candidate, the main wall sits 0.55 m behind the footprint front edge; lower and upper bay faces advance to roughly 0.03 m and 0.00 m from the source edge; the narrow right bay sits about 0.13 m back; the left entry back wall is 1.15 m recessed. The terrace deck is modeled at 9.20 m above the driveway datum, railing top at 10.45 m, and pergola header at 12.35 m within the 12.625 m source height envelope. These are photo-proportioned candidate subdivisions, not survey dimensions.

## What v026 changes
The front four metres of prior 1040 placeholder/rejected facade material are removed where source-bounded, then rebuilt as shell geometry:
- recessed main wall and entry;
- street-level garage plane;
- two distinct projecting central bays with real side returns and soffit/slab surfaces;

- blue side returns so the front no longer reads as cardboard;
- solid timbered terrace fascia;
- shallow terrace deck;
- sparse open front/side railing;

Fine timber pattern, decorative metal railing loops, bougainvillea, full side/rear architecture, interiors and final rear/roof massing remain deferred.
