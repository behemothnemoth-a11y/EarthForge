# Redfield POC 001 — L1 Truth v005

v005 is the first EarthForge pass whose main question is:

> Does this look like **Redfield**, rather than merely a plausible small-town block?

It remains normal-block-only.

## v004 review targets addressed

The Minecraft v004 walkaround exposed five major issues:

1. too many facades still shared the same storefront grammar;
2. the roofscape was dominated by broad, uniform dark rectangles;
3. City Hall did not read strongly enough as a landmark;
4. rear/alley elevations remained generic boxes;
5. the 621/623/625-627 historic north end needed much more specific treatment.

v005 attacks all five at once.

## What v005 adds

### Building-specific facade programs

Every Main-facing footprint gets its own explicit facade program:

- bay count;
- door positions;
- lower glazing rhythm;
- upper-window count and width;
- sign/transom band;
- awning coverage;
- parapet type;
- side-window treatment;
- rear service-door/window program;
- roof-equipment program.

This replaces the v004 "same grammar, different colors" approach.

### North-end photo truth

A 2023 Redfield downtown photo clearly shows the historic group that includes:

- Carpets Plus;
- the pale-green neighboring storefront;
- the Dakota Tan/Hair storefront.

v005 uses that streetscape as a direct visual reference for the 621/623/625
sequence: red-brick upper floors, differentiated lower storefronts, awnings,
decorative cornices, and a taller historic skyline.

### 625 / 627 split

OSM represents the northern west-side mass as one footprint.

v005 keeps the real footprint, but splits the Main facade and roofline into two
apparent buildings:

- 625 on the southern half;
- 627 on the northern half.

An internal dividing wall is added to stop the pair from reading as one giant
store.

### City Hall

626 is treated as a historic bank/civic facade:

- symmetrical multi-bay front;
- sandstone/stone base and trim;
- taller entrance surround;
- stronger pilasters;
- raised central parapet;
- differentiated cornice;
- roof access/mechanical details.

This is still a block-scale interpretation, not a surveyed elevation drawing.

### Roofscape

Roofs now include building-specific combinations of:

- stepped parapets;
- crest parapets;
- chimneys;
- vent stacks;
- HVAC boxes;
- skylights;
- roof-access boxes;
- rear roof-height transitions.

### Rear / alley elevations

Primary buildings now receive:

- rear service doors;
- small service windows;
- loading/service canopies;
- rear parapets;
- occasional vents/chimneys.

Rear additions stay lower and simpler.

### Streetscape

- diagonal Main Street parking lines;
- curb slabs;
- less-uniform lamp placement;
- benches and planters;
- sidewalk-edge variation.

## Evidence policy

Each facade is tagged as one of:

- `photo_supported`
- `history_supported`
- `inferred`

Photo-supported does **not** mean millimeter-perfect. It means the facade
program was materially informed by a visible exterior reference.

## Still locked

- Microblocks;
- interiors;
- text-bearing signs;
- custom textures;
- claimed survey-grade heights.

Those remain outside L1 Truth.
