# Lombard Curb v009

Curb-only pass around approved v008 road. The road geometry and road materials are locked and validated unchanged. The v008 running-bond RGB paver road is carried forward unchanged. The curb follows SF Public Works Standard Plan 87,169 as a 6-inch nominal curb quantized to the 1/16m Astra grid: 2-cell top width, 2-cell exposed height, and a 3-cell lower body approximating the standard batter. No terrain, stairs, retaining walls, vegetation, buildings, or other context are present.
