# Lombard Retaining Walls v012

Retaining-wall-only pass on approved v011. Every occupied v011 road, curb, terrace-base, and local edge-height cell is locked and validated unchanged. Retaining walls are added only where the smoothed LiDAR terrace sits more than 0.50 m above the standard curb. The wall rises to the LiDAR-supported terrace level with light concrete faces and a thin smooth-stone cap. No stairs, railings, hedges/flowers, buildings, broad terrain, or outer street context are added.
