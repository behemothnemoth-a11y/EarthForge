# Lombard Terrace Base v010

Terrace-base-only pass outside approved v009 road and curb. Every occupied v009 road/curb cell is locked and validated unchanged. The immediate terrace/planter masses come from the DataSF crooked-block ROW, clipped to a 4.5 m support band around the road, with elevations from the 2010 NOAA/USGS class-2 LiDAR grid smoothed over about 1.5 m to suppress scan noise. Only a shallow soil shelf and minimal exposed edge face are included. No stairs, detailed retaining walls, hedges/flowers, buildings, broad terrain, or outer street context are present.
